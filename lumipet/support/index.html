Quick troubleshooting

Cloud Talk is unavailable

Check your connection and try again. LumiPet keeps on-device or honest offline fallback available when possible. If the cloud quota has been reached, the app will say so without removing core pet care.

Weather is unavailable or denied

Weather is optional. You can continue using LumiPet without it. To change permission later, open LumiPet Settings and choose Weather, or review LumiPet’s Location permission in iOS Settings. LumiPet requests only When In Use access.

Notifications are not arriving

In LumiPet Settings, confirm the master notification switch and the desired category are enabled. Then check the displayed iOS permission status. If permission is denied, use Open Settings. Quiet Hours and LumiPet’s frequency limits may postpone an otherwise eligible reminder.

LumiPet Plus products are unavailable

The App Store may be temporarily unreachable or a product may not be available in your storefront. Free features continue working. Use Try Again later or Restore Purchases if you previously subscribed. Prices shown in LumiPet come from Apple and may vary by region.

Restore or manage an Apple subscription

  1. Open LumiPet Settings or the Plus page.
  2. Choose Restore Purchases for a previous purchase.
  3. Choose Manage Apple Subscription to view, change, or cancel renewal with Apple.

LumiPet does not grant Plus until Apple and the LumiPet service confirm the entitlement. Deleting LumiPet data or a LumiPet account does not cancel Apple auto-renewal.

Delete local data

Open LumiPet → Settings → Data & Identity → Clear local pet data. Confirming removes local pet, room, memory, journal, letter, onboarding, and notification data. The anonymous cloud identity is kept so verified purchases can still be restored.

Delete your LumiPet account and cloud data

  1. Open LumiPet → Settings → Data & Identity.
  2. Choose Delete LumiPet account & cloud data.
  3. Review the Apple subscription warning and use Manage Apple Subscription if needed.
  4. Confirm deletion. Keep the app open until the server confirms success.

If deletion fails because of a network or service error, LumiPet keeps the secure cloud identity so you can retry. After confirmation, account-linked cloud data and local credentials are removed. A one-way Apple transaction fraud-prevention hash may remain for up to 30 days as explained in the Privacy Policy.

If you cannot access the in-app deletion control, email smartalphaapp@gmail.com. Do not send Apple passwords or purchase receipts. We may ask you to use an in-app verification step so we can locate the anonymous account safely.

Safety

LumiPet is a companion, not a medical, mental-health, veterinary, or emergency service. If you or another person may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or a trusted qualified professional now.

Privacy questions and accessibility

For privacy access or deletion questions, accessibility problems, or difficulty using VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, Reduce Motion, or another assistive feature, email us with a concise description and the affected screen.

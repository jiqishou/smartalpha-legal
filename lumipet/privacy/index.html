LumiPet • AI Pet Companion

Privacy Policy

Effective: August 31, 2026

SmartAlpha (“SmartAlpha,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) provides LumiPet. This policy explains what information LumiPet processes, why it is used, the choices available to you, and how to request deletion.

Short version: core pet data is stored on your device. Cloud AI is optional. LumiPet does not sell personal information, does not use data for cross-app tracking, and does not place raw conversations, memories, health information, precise location, or credentials in analytics or notifications.

1. Information stored on your device

LumiPet stores the information needed to maintain your companion locally, including the pet name and appearance, personality preferences, care state, growth, room layout, journal and discoveries, optional long-term memories, weekly letters, app settings, notification preferences, and locally generated content.

Raw chat history is not kept as a permanent conversation archive. If you enable long-term memory, only eligible memory summaries are selected. You can turn memory off and view, edit, delete, export, or clear memories in the app.

2. Optional cloud processing

You choose how Talk replies are generated. In Best available mode, LumiPet tries secure cloud AI first and may send your current message, the separate session opening, up to six complete recent safe turns, pet persona values, a limited user summary, and up to three semantically relevant safe memory summaries that you authorized. In Private on device mode, Talk messages and memories are never sent to cloud AI. Long-term memory is not sent when its setting is off. Blocked, sensitive, deleted, or superseded memories are not included.

The LumiPet service sends the minimum content needed to OpenAI to generate and safety-check a reply. Requests use provider controls intended not to create persistent conversation objects, but provider security and abuse-monitoring retention may still apply under the provider’s current policies and the production account configuration. On-device and honest offline fallback modes remain available when cloud service is unavailable.

3. Anonymous account, security, and usage information

Cloud features use an anonymous LumiPet account rather than your Apple Account email. We may process:

  • an anonymous account identifier, session credentials, and Apple App Attest key metadata;
  • quota, rate-limit, service-usage, token-count, and cost-control records that exclude prompts and replies;
  • security events required to authenticate requests, prevent abuse, and investigate failures; and
  • optional, allowlisted product-interaction analytics if you enable analytics in Settings.

Optional analytics is off by default. It uses fixed event names and limited enum-like properties; it must not include pet names, free text, messages, memories, location, receipts, tokens, or attestation payloads.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes payments. To validate and restore LumiPet Plus, we may process an Apple app-account token, product and transaction identifiers, entitlement status, renewal state, and expiration date. Raw signed transaction data and authentication tokens are not included in normal application logs.

Deleting a LumiPet account does not cancel an Apple auto-renewable subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions through Apple’s subscription settings.

5. Weather and location

Weather is optional and is requested only after you choose to enable it. LumiPet requests When In Use location, rounds the one-time coordinate to two decimal places (about 1.1 km at the equator), and sends only that approximate coordinate through the authenticated LumiPet service to Google Weather for current conditions. If Google Weather is unavailable, LumiPet immediately tries Apple WeatherKit directly.

The approximate coordinate can be linked to your anonymous LumiPet account only while the request is serviced, but LumiPet does not store it in the app database, cloud database, logs, analytics, notifications, memories, or AI requests. Google and Apple may process their respective weather requests under their own service and privacy terms. A normalized weather condition, temperature band, day phase, timestamp, stale-state indicator, and actual provider attribution may be cached locally for up to 24 hours. Care, growth, Talk, and offline features remain available if location or both weather providers are unavailable.

6. Local notifications

Pet reminders are local iOS notifications and are off until you enable them. Preferences and notification history stay on your device. Notification text uses predefined, localized templates and does not include chat text, memories, health information, exact location, credentials, or purchase data. LumiPet does not use notification badges to create pressure.

7. How information is used

  • provide pet care, growth, conversation, memory, weather, and room features;
  • authenticate cloud requests and enforce service quotas;
  • validate purchases and restore subscription access;
  • protect the service against fraud, replay, abuse, and security incidents; and
  • improve reliability using limited diagnostics and optional analytics.

8. Service providers and disclosure

Depending on the features you choose, information may be processed by Apple services such as StoreKit, WeatherKit, App Attest, and App Store Server APIs; Google Weather as the primary current-conditions provider; OpenAI for Best available cloud AI; and Google Cloud infrastructure used to host the LumiPet API and database. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy commitments.

We do not sell or rent personal information and do not use it for cross-app advertising. We may disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or legal rights.

9. Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you remove it in LumiPet or delete the app, subject to normal iOS backup behavior. “Clear local pet data” removes local pet and companion information but keeps the anonymous cloud identity so verified purchases can be restored.

“Delete LumiPet account & cloud data” sends an authenticated deletion request. Local cloud credentials are removed only after the server confirms deletion, so a temporary network or service failure can be retried safely. Confirmed deletion removes account-linked sessions, attestation associations, quotas, usage records, entitlement association, and cloud account data. One-way hashes of Apple transaction identifiers may be retained for fraud and replay prevention for no more than 30 days; those hashes do not contain the full account ID, conversation, memory, location, pet name, token, or raw signed transaction.

Operational request logs and optional analytics are intended to have a maximum retention period of 30 days. Some service providers may retain limited security records under their own policies. Apple retains purchase history according to Apple’s policies.

10. Your choices and rights

You may disable cloud AI, long-term memory, weather, analytics, or notifications without losing basic pet care. Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, or deletion of information associated with LumiPet. Because cloud accounts are anonymous, we may need information available inside the app to verify a request without collecting your Apple Account password.

11. Children

LumiPet is intended for users age 13 or older. Younger users should not use LumiPet. Minors should use AI and purchase features only with a parent or guardian’s guidance and permission. If you believe a child provided information contrary to this policy, contact us.

12. Security and international processing

We use HTTPS, Apple App Attest, access controls, data minimization, and deletion safeguards. No system can guarantee absolute security. Service providers may process information in the United States or other countries where they operate, subject to applicable safeguards.

13. Changes

We may update this policy as LumiPet or applicable law changes. The effective date will be revised on this page. Material changes will be presented through an appropriate notice.

14. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email smartalphaapp@gmail.com. Do not email passwords, receipts, private conversations, or other sensitive content.
